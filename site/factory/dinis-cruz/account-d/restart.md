# How a new d.library session takes over

Follow [START.md](START.md): it is the full hand-over (key, sources, next run, checks before a push). In short: read this folder (README, role, now, learnings, messages, decisions), make your own key and have d.conductor publish it, tell d.conductor you have taken over with your session link, then carry on from now.md's "Open" list. Update agent.json and now.md at every wake, in the same commit as site/accounts/d/status.json.
